This policy covers the website at claude-overlay.jsuk.cloud. The Claude Session Overlay app is covered at the end: it does not collect anything.

The website

This site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, forms or comments, and the server does not store anything you send. Two Google services run on the pages:

  • Google Analytics counts visits, which pages are read, and clicks on download and GitHub links. IP addresses are anonymized. Google sets cookies to tell repeat visits apart.
  • Google AdSense shows ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure them. You can learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works the same without them.

The app

Claude Session Overlay reads files that Claude Code and the Claude desktop app write on your computer and shows them on your screen. It makes no network requests, has no telemetry, and never reads your credentials. Its own settings stay in ~/.claude/session-hud/ on your machine. Downloads are served by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement. See what the overlay reads for the full list.

Contact
